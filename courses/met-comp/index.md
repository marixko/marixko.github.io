This course was designed for graduate students (master's and doctoral) in Astronomy at Observatório Nacional. Here I assume the student has seen little or no statistics during their undergraduate studies. So I begin the course with a very gentle introduction, which then accelerates in complexity due to the number of interesting topics that need to be covered in a single semester. The student is therefore expected to take time to study the topics in more depth.
In short, these notes are meant to be a reference for you to consult later, whenever you need them in your research.

I hope the course is useful to you in some way!
