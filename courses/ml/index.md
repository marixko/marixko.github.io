This course was designed for graduate students (master's and doctoral) in Astronomy at Observatório Nacional, largely because of the growing trend of using Artificial Intelligence in scientific research.

Here I plan to show you that Artificial Intelligence is neither a seven-headed monster nor an impossible-to-decipher black box. To do that, I will need to go somewhat deep into the mathematics.

My goal is that in this course you learn the mathematical foundations of machine learning and understand what is happening behind your command line. This will therefore not be a merely introductory course, and it will require some basic knowledge of vectors, linear algebra, probability and statistics.

I hope the course is useful to you in some way!
